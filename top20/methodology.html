How the Dailies are ranked

669 of 669 published Dailies evaluated. These are provisional AI-assisted first-pass editorial scores, not objective quality measures or a creator-approved final ranking.

Artistic merit: 100-point scale

Each criterion is scored from 0–10 and weighted as follows. The artistic score is the sum of each rating multiplied by its weight divided by 10.

CriterionWeightEvidence
Visual impact20%Individually assessed from each published image preview: lighting, composition, impact, atmosphere.
Style15%Individually assessed from each published image preview: visual language, cohesion, palette and treatment.
Story15%Individually assessed from each published image preview: emotion, implied action, character and narrative.
Originality15%Individually assessed from each published image preview: inventive concepts and interpretation.
Execution estimate15%Calculated estimate from the visual-impact and style ratings; not a separate high-resolution inspection.
Editorial value estimate10%Calculated estimate from story, originality and subject classifications from the Sitemap.
Zine identity estimate5%Calculated estimate from style, originality, story and subject metadata.
Banner suitability estimate5%Calculated estimate from impact, style and story; there is no aspect-ratio exclusion in the Top 20.

Popularity means popularity of the theme

Theme appeal is a separate 0–5 editorial estimate of a subject’s general audience reach, cultural recognition, cross-audience interest and evergreen value. It is not the number of views or reactions for an artwork. It never increases artistic merit and is used only to break exact artistic-score ties. No audience analytics are claimed.

Sources and limitations

Publication metadata, titles, tags, post links and image URLs come exclusively from the public Zine Sitemap. All original 669 Dailies were considered without an artistic shortlist. The four individually assessed criteria were judged from the published 225 × 150 previews. An earlier subset of 81 was inspected at medium size and 44 published originals were independently checked, but not all 669 full-size images underwent technical review. Other criterion scores are clearly identified as estimates, and creator review is still pending.

Older 4:3 work and special Dailies are eligible for this ranking. The separate YouTube-banner requirement for 3:2 artwork does not affect the Top 20.

Updates and reproducibility

Each assessment is associated with its canonical Zine post and published original image URL. A replaced image does not automatically inherit an old score. New entries are not silently assigned scores. The same scoring formula is applied to every reviewed Daily.
